## ABOUT

I find friction in operations and workflows, break it down into flows, decisions and data, and turn it into small tools and prototypes.

My focus is not a specific framework, but making confusing work easier to understand, operate and improve.

Operations support PWA that guides frontline troubleshooting and turns response history into improvement data.

`React` `TypeScript` `PWA` `PHP` `MySQL`

[Demo](https://ryohei0otsuka.github.io/trouble-diner/)

### [KNOT FOUND](https://github.com/Ryohei0Otsuka/knot-found)

`Electron` `React` `TypeScript` `Crawler` `Tests`

[Releases](https://github.com/Ryohei0Otsuka/knot-found/releases)

### [CSVjoiner](https://github.com/Ryohei0Otsuka/CSVjoiner)

Desktop tool for JOIN / SPLIT / TRANSFORM workflows without repeatedly opening CSV files in Excel.

`Python` `pandas` `Tkinter` `pytest` `PyInstaller`

[Releases](https://github.com/Ryohei0Otsuka/CSVjoiner/releases)

### [Pathless Map](https://github.com/Ryohei0Otsuka/pathless-map)

`React` `TypeScript` `Local-first`
